# Brag Plan: Milkman

## What is this app?
Milkman is a farm-fresh dairy delivery platform delivering 100% pure Vedic Desi Gir Cow & Murrah Buffalo milk straight to doorsteps every morning with seamless subscriptions and batch-video transparency.

## The angle
The morning dairy delivery problem is solved with radical freshness and proof. Instead of mysterious plastic packets with preservatives, Milkman provides farm-fresh pure milk in sanitized glass bottles before you wake up, backed by daily QR batch video verification.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A cinematic alarm clock / sunrise wake-up moment:
"What if fresh, pure farm milk arrived at your doorstep before your alarm even went off?"

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Purity & Choice**: Desi Gir Cow Vedic Milk & Murrah Buffalo Milk (₹28 / 500ml, zero preservatives, farm-fresh).
- **Batch Video Trace**: Transparent QR batch video verification — customers can see actual milking and testing of their daily batch!
- **Effortless Subscription**: Pause, resume, and manage daily milk drops effortlessly on mobile.

## Outro / punchline
"Farm fresh milk, every morning. You wake up. We've already delivered."
Final Milkman brand lockup with download / book CTA.

## User flow worth showing
Select Vedic Milk Plan (Cow/Buffalo) → 5:30 AM doorstep delivery before waking up → Trace Batch Video QR for 100% purity check.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: Fresh, vibrant morning dairy launch
- Interpretation: Upbeat, crisp, trustworthy, and modern with lively card transitions and warm golden/emerald lighting.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 18 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#0A0F1D` to `#0F172A` deep navy with `#F0FDF4` / `#FFFFFF` frosted glass cards
- Accent: `#00A854` (Fresh Emerald Green) & `#F59E0B` (Golden Vedic Sun)
- Text: `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`
- Display font: 'Outfit', sans-serif
- Body font: 'Outfit', system-ui, sans-serif
- Strongest visual elements: 3D plan cards (Desi Gir Cow & Murrah Buffalo), glass bottles, green purity badges, QR batch trace badge.

## Share copy (draft)
We built @MilkmanSite — 100% pure farm-fresh milk delivered to your doorstep before you wake up, with daily batch video verification. Start your morning pure! 🥛🌱

## Audio direction
- Role: Warm, upbeat rhythmic bed with motion-matched UI accents
- Music: `assets/music/track.mp3` (Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol 1)
- Music treatment: Starts at 0.0s, peaks during plan showcase, smooth 1.5s fade out at end
- SFX posture: Crisp motion-matched UI clicks, swooshes, and bell chimes
- Restraint rule: Audio should feel crisp, fresh, and friendly; no jarring EDM drops.
